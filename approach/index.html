How we think

Five layers.

Safety is not one thing you buy. It is five layers, and each one buys the next one time. Walk down the page and watch them light.

LAYER 01

Deter

Make it obvious this place is ready. Lighting, sightlines, visible controls, posture and presence. Most trouble goes where it is easiest; the first layer makes sure that is not here.

We doSite walk, perimeter and entry review, visitor-management recommendations.

LAYER 02

Detect

See it early, from where you are. Cameras that are watched, alarms that mean something, staff who know what unusual looks like and have a way to say so.

We doCamera and alarm coverage review, reporting pathways, threat-assessment team setup.

LAYER 03

Delay

Buy the minutes that matter. Controlled entries, locked classrooms and offices, interior doors that hold, a building that slows a threat long enough for people to move and responders to arrive.

We doAccess-control and door-hardware review, secure-entry design, lockdown procedures.

LAYER 04

Respond

Everyone knows their job. A plan people have actually practiced. Who calls, who moves, who locks, who talks to the responders, and how it all gets communicated in the first ninety seconds.

We doEmergency operations plans, drills and tabletop exercises, staff training, law-enforcement coordination.

LAYER 05

Recover

Get back to normal, on purpose. Reunification, communication with families and the public, support for the people involved, and an honest look at what to change.

We doReunification planning, after-action review, continuity planning.
