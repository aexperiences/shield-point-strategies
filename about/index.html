Founder & Principal

Brian Asmus

30 years in law enforcement. 20 of them as Chief of Police. All of them in the business of keeping people safe.

Led the Liberty Lake Police Department from its founding through two decades, building the department, its K-9 program and its community-policing model. When the City of Liberty Lake stood up its own police department, Brian was the chief who built it — the officers, the technology, the K-9 program, and a community-policing approach that treated education and visibility as seriously as enforcement.

In 2020 he retired from the department and took on a different front line: he led school safety and security for Central Valley School District from September 2020 — a district of fourteen thousand five hundred students across Spokane Valley and Liberty Lake.

He also served as the Director of Physical Security for a large healthcare provider.

Public service

  • Spokane Valley Fire Department Commissioner
  • Served as Chair of the Spokane Regional Emergency Communications Board
  • Charter member and past president of the Liberty Lake Centennial Rotary Club
  • Serves on the Liberty Lake Police Foundation Board

Education

Memberships & federal service

  • Served on a DEA task force
  • Served as law enforcement liaison with the Secret Service Presidential Protective Division
