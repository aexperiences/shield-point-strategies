Founder & Principal

Brian Asmus

30 years in law enforcement. 20 of them as Chief of Police. All of them in the business of keeping people safe.

Led the Liberty Lake Police Department from its founding through two decades, building the department, its K-9 program and its community-policing model. When the City of Liberty Lake stood up its own police department, Brian was the chief who built it — the officers, the technology, the K-9 program, and a community-policing approach that treated education and visibility as seriously as enforcement.

In 2020 he retired from the department to take on a different front line: School Safety & Security Supervisor, Central Valley School District (since September 2020) — safety and security for a district of 14,500 students across Spokane Valley and Liberty Lake.

Police officer, City of Yelm, Washington — eleven years before Liberty Lake.

Serving alongside

  • Liberty Lake Fire Commissioner
  • Vice Chair, Spokane Regional Emergency Communications board
  • Founding member and past president, Rotary Club of Liberty Lake

Education

  • B.A., Organizational Leadership — Patten University
  • B.A., Business — Pacific Lutheran University
