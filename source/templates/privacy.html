When you contact us

If you fill in the contact form, what you type (your name, email, phone, organization and message) goes to {{ b.principal }} at {{ b.name }} so he can answer you. It is kept in his office records. It is never sold, rented or shared with anyone else.

The readiness check runs on your own device. Your answers are not sent anywhere unless you choose to text or email the result to us.

What the site counts

The site counts visits: which pages were opened and roughly where visitors come from. It does not collect your name or email, and it sets no advertising cookies. To tell a returning visitor apart, it keeps a random number in your own browser. You can clear it any time by clearing your browser's site data.

The lettering on the site comes from Google Fonts, so your browser asks Google for the font files.
